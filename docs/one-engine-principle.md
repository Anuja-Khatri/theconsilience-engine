# One Engine, Four Config Points

The same engine serves every use case. No fork. No second codebase. The verticals differ in template and buyer, not machinery.

## The four config points

### 1. Who selects the inputs

- **Research demo:** the builder selected six theories
- **Open researcher workspace:** the researcher names their theories
- **Biopharma flow:** the customer names their mechanistic thesis and its rivals
- **Diligence flow:** the target company's published thesis is set against its rival frameworks

### 2. Whose instruments define testability

- **Research demo:** the field's instruments
- **Biopharma flow:** the customer's own assays and capabilities
- **Diligence flow:** the financing round's stated milestones

### 3. What the output looks like

- **Research demo:** four interactive screens
- **Biopharma flow:** a board-ready landscape
- **Diligence flow:** a per-deal report

### 4. Which domain pack the ontology runs

- **Natively:** mind and brain sciences
- **Available:** a biomedical domain pack
- **Extensible:** any domain where a domain pack has been authored

## Nothing else changes

The extraction pipeline is the same. The typed graphs are the same. The VF2 matching is the same. The grading ladder is the same. The equivocation test is the same. The decidability check is the same. The provenance manifest is the same.

One engine. Four config points. Every vertical is a configuration, not a rebuild.
