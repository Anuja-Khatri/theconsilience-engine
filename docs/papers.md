# Published Papers

All four papers are published on Zenodo with permanent DOIs.

## Paper 1: Deterministic Verification for Cross-Domain Scientific Claim Comparison

The flagship paper. Full architecture description, V1 embedding system case study (6,341 false positives), V2 neurosymbolic rebuild, COGITATE blind test evaluation. Cites Kambhampati's LLM Modulo as the governing pattern.

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23086966](https://zenodo.org/records/23086966)

## Paper 2: Per-Instance Lexical Ablation as a Grading Mechanism

The equivocation test paper. Newman's 1928 vulnerability. Typed subgraph isomorphism worked example on two competing theories. VF2 mapping attempt showing which nodes matched and which edges broke. Pseudocode for the equivocation removal test. Before-and-after ablation showing what survives when shared terms are stripped. Connection to molecular substructure search in cheminformatics.

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23119443](https://zenodo.org/records/23119443)

## Paper 3: Computational Testability Assessment

The decidability paper. Versioned instrument capability map. Three verdict types with structural reasons. Substrate disagreement walkthrough showing the system correctly ruling a question untestable. Discussion of Focused Research Organisations as the natural buyer of this output. Genuinely novel: no prior work on computational testability assessment for cross-domain disagreements exists.

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23119362](https://zenodo.org/records/23119362)

## Paper 4: Beyond the Transformer for Scientific Discovery

Practitioner's survey of six alternative architecture camps. For each: what they propose, what we adopted, what we deferred, and why. Documents the decision to build on Marcus (neurosymbolic), Kambhampati (LLM Modulo), and Bengio (generator-estimator) while deferring Chollet (program synthesis), LeCun (JEPA), and studying Scholkopf (causal representation learning) as an evaluation standard.

**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23121190](https://zenodo.org/records/23121190)
